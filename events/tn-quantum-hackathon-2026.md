# TN Quantum Hackathon 2026

Chattanooga, November 13 to 15, 2026. Hosted by the Chattanooga Quantum Collaborative and CO.LAB.

- **Award:** the qBraid Award for the most creative use of qBraid, open to every team in every track
- **Prize:** $200 in gift cards, split evenly across the winning team, plus 1,000 qBraid credits for each member
- **Enter:** fill in the entry form ([Alex to add: form link]) by the **Sunday 9:00am** deadline. It needs two or three slides on how you used qBraid, plus optional extras like a screen recording or qBraid link; see the [README](../README.md#how-to-enter)
- **Judging:** qBraid judges Sunday morning, separately from track judging ([`JUDGING.md`](../JUDGING.md))
- **Announced:** at the closing ceremony at The Landing Social House, during winners and prizes
- **Hardware:** teams can run on real quantum hardware through qBraid, including Quantinuum. Quantinuum asks each participant to create a Nexus account and accept its terms first, which takes under a minute.

Every participant who completes the weekend also gets a qBraid completion certificate with a verification link.
