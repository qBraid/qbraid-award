# TN Quantum Hackathon 2026

Chattanooga, November 13 to 15, 2026. Hosted by the Chattanooga Quantum Collaborative and CO.LAB.

- **Award:** the qBraid Award for the most creative use of qBraid, open to every team in every track
- **Prize:** a gift card of about $200 plus qBraid credits ([Alex to confirm: credit amount per team member])
- **Format:** up to three slides after your track presentation, with "qBraid Award" in each slide title. Follow [`SHOWCASE_TEMPLATE.md`](../SHOWCASE_TEMPLATE.md).
- **How to enter:** submit the slides with your final project materials by the **Sunday 9:00am** deadline, and [Alex to confirm with CO.LAB: the checkbox or field in the submission portal]
- **Judging:** two qBraid judges score the entries Sunday morning, separately from track judging, using [`JUDGING.md`](../JUDGING.md). Teams in the top three may get a two-minute visit before track judging starts at 10:30am.
- **Announced:** at the closing ceremony at The Landing Social House, during winners and prizes
- **Hardware:** teams can run on real quantum hardware through qBraid, including Quantinuum. Quantinuum asks each participant to create a Nexus account and accept its terms first, which takes under a minute.

Every participant who completes the weekend also gets a qBraid completion certificate with a verification link.
