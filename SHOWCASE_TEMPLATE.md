# qBraid Award showcase template

Your showcase has three parts:

- **In-person events:** make each part one slide, put the three slides after your track presentation, and put "qBraid Award" in each slide title.
- **Remote events:** copy this file into your repo as `QBRAID_SHOWCASE.md` and fill in the three sections.

Judges should be able to get through it in three minutes. To see what finished slides look like, open [`template/slides-preview.html`](./template/slides-preview.html) in a browser.

---

## 1. What qBraid did for us

- **Our project in one sentence:** <what you built and for whom>
- **Where each step ran:** a simple left-to-right diagram, for example: notebook in qBraid Lab, then a simulator, then quantum hardware. Name the actual devices, simulators, frameworks or tools.
- **What qBraid made possible:** <one sentence on what you couldn't have done on a laptop alone>

## 2. Our best qBraid moment

- **One visual:** a chart, plot or screenshot of the result you're proudest of. Comparisons work well: simulator against hardware, one device against another, small circuits against large ones.
- **The takeaway:** <one or two sentences someone outside quantum can follow>

## 3. Check it

- **qBraid job IDs:** <copy them from the Jobs page in your qBraid account, or print `job.id` in your notebook>
- **Devices and simulators used:** <list>
- **Credits used:** <roughly how many, and how you kept the number low>
- **Run it yourself:** <link to your repo or notebook>. Add a Launch on qBraid button to your repo's README so judges can open it in one click:

  ```markdown
  [<img src="https://qbraid-static.s3.amazonaws.com/logos/Launch_on_qBraid_white.png" width="150">](https://account.qbraid.com?gitHubUrl=https://github.com/<your-username>/<your-repo>.git)
  ```

- **Next:** <what you'd try next on qBraid>

---

## Tips

- One clear chart beats three busy ones.
- Show something real that ran, even if the result was noisy. Explaining the noise honestly scores well.
- Stop at three slides. Judges don't read past them.
